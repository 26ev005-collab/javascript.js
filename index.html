Welcome to SharePlate

Welcome to the Food Wastage Management System. Our main aim is to reduce food wastage and help people who are in need of food.

Every day, large amounts of food are wasted. Instead of throwing away extra food, we can share it with poor and needy people.

🍎 Save Food, Save Lives 🍎

About Us

Food Wastage Management is a system that helps collect surplus food from homes, hotels, restaurants and other organisations.

The collected food can be distributed to orphanages, old age homes and people who need food support.

Our Main Objectives

  • Reduce food wastage.
  • Help poor and needy people.
  • Encourage food donation.
  • Protect the environment.
  • Create awareness about food wastage.

Our Services

1. Food Donation

People can donate extra food instead of throwing it away.

2. Food Collection

Surplus food can be collected from hotels, restaurants and houses.

3. Food Distribution

Collected food can be distributed to needy people and charitable organisations.

4. Food Awareness

We create awareness about reducing food wastage and saving food.
